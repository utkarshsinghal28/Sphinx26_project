## What is PMFBY

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana is the national crop insurance scheme. It protects farmers against
loss of yield due to natural calamities, pests and disease, so that a bad season does not push a
family into debt.

## Premium paid by the farmer

- Kharif food and oilseed crops: 2% of the sum insured.
- Rabi food and oilseed crops: 1.5% of the sum insured.
- Annual commercial and horticultural crops: 5% of the sum insured.
- The rest of the premium is subsidised by the central and state government.

## Who can enrol

- All farmers growing a notified crop in a notified area, with or without a loan.
- Loanee farmers are enrolled automatically by their bank. Non-loanee farmers must enrol
  themselves before the cut-off date.
- Enrolment is done at the bank, at a Common Service Centre, or on the PMFBY portal.

## How to claim after crop loss

1. Report the loss within 72 hours to the insurance company, the bank, or the district agriculture
   office. You can report through the Crop Insurance App or the toll-free number 14447.
2. Keep the acknowledgement number. Do not lose it.
3. The loss is surveyed by the insurance company and often verified by a joint committee.
4. The claim is transferred directly to the bank account linked at enrolment.

## Documents needed

- Aadhaar card and bank passbook
- Land records or tenancy proof
- Sowing certificate / self-declaration of crop sown
- For localised damage: photographs of the affected crop with date and location

## Important points

- Enrolment after the cut-off date is not allowed. Dates are announced per state and per season on
  pmfby.gov.in.
- Report the loss immediately. Late reporting is the single biggest reason claims are rejected.
- The claim amount depends on the assessed yield loss and the sum insured, not on the cost of
  cultivation.

## हिंदी में सारांश

- प्रधानमंत्री फसल बीमा योजना से फसल खराब होने पर मुआवजा मिलता है।
- किसान को प्रीमियम का बहुत कम हिस्सा देना होता है: खरीफ में 2 प्रतिशत, रबी में 1.5 प्रतिशत।
- फसल खराब होने पर 72 घंटे के अंदर सूचना देना जरूरी है।
- शिकायत के लिए टोल-फ्री नंबर 14447 पर कॉल करें या Crop Insurance App इस्तेमाल करें।
- बीमा कराने की आखिरी तारीख के बाद नामांकन नहीं हो सकता।
- आधार, बैंक पासबुक, जमीन के कागजात और बोई गई फसल का प्रमाण जरूरी है।
