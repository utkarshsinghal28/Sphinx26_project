## What is PM-KISAN

PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) is a central government income support scheme for
landholding farmer families. The government pays Rs 6,000 per year directly into the farmer's bank
account, in three equal instalments of Rs 2,000 each, every four months.

## Who is eligible

- All landholding farmer families, small and marginal as well as large.
- The land must be recorded in the family's name in government land records.
- The scheme is per family, not per person. Husband, wife and minor children count as one family.
- Excluded: institutional landholders, income tax payers, serving or retired government employees
  (except Group D and multi-tasking staff), professionals like doctors, engineers and lawyers who
  are registered with professional bodies, and pensioners drawing more than Rs 10,000 per month.

## Benefits

- Rs 6,000 per year paid directly to the bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
- Paid in three instalments of Rs 2,000. Instalments are announced on the PM-KISAN portal.
- No middleman and no application fee.

## How to apply

1. Go to the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or the local revenue office / Patwari / Talathi.
2. The officer enters your Aadhaar number, land record details and bank account number.
3. Self-registration is also available on pmkisan.gov.in under the "New Farmer Registration" tab.
4. Complete e-KYC. This is mandatory. e-KYC can be done by Aadhaar OTP on the portal, or
   biometric authentication at a CSC.
5. After approval the name appears in the beneficiary list and payment starts from the next
   instalment cycle.

## Documents needed

- Aadhaar card
- Land ownership records (7/12 extract in Maharashtra, khatauni in UP, jamabandi in Rajasthan)
- Bank account passbook (account must be Aadhaar-seeded)
- Mobile number linked to Aadhaar

## Common problems

- If the payment has stopped, the most common reason is incomplete e-KYC. Complete e-KYC and the
  payment resumes from the next instalment.
- Check payment status on pmkisan.gov.in under "Beneficiary Status" using Aadhaar number or
  registered mobile number.
- If land records do not match, contact the local revenue office for correction.

## हिंदी में सारांश

- पीएम-किसान योजना में सरकार किसान परिवार को हर साल 6,000 रुपये देती है।
- यह राशि 2,000 रुपये की तीन किस्तों में सीधे बैंक खाते में आती है।
- जमीन सरकारी रिकॉर्ड में परिवार के नाम पर होनी चाहिए। एक परिवार को एक ही लाभ मिलता है।
- आवेदन के लिए आधार कार्ड, जमीन के कागजात और बैंक पासबुक चाहिए।
- ई-केवाईसी (e-KYC) करना जरूरी है। बिना ई-केवाईसी किस्त नहीं आती।
- किस्त की स्थिति pmkisan.gov.in पर "Beneficiary Status" में देख सकते हैं। हेल्पलाइन 155261।
