## What is PM-JAY

Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana is a health insurance scheme for poor and
vulnerable families. It pays for hospital treatment so that illness does not destroy a family's
savings.

## Benefits

- Up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospital care.
- Cashless treatment at empanelled public and private hospitals.
- Covers hospitalisation, day care surgery, medicines and diagnostics during the stay.
- No cap on family size, age or gender. Pre-existing diseases are covered from day one.
- There is no waiting period.

## Who is eligible

- Families identified as deprived rural households in the SECC 2011 database, including
  Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe households, landless labourers, and families living in
  kuchha houses.
- Occupational categories of urban workers such as rag pickers, street vendors and construction
  workers.
- All citizens aged 70 years and above, regardless of income (from the 2024 expansion).
- To check your name, use the "Am I Eligible" tool on pmjay.gov.in or call 14555.

## How to get the Ayushman Card

1. Check eligibility on pmjay.gov.in or at any Common Service Centre.
2. Visit a CSC or the Ayushman Mitra desk at an empanelled hospital with Aadhaar.
3. Complete e-KYC with Aadhaar and a photograph.
4. The Ayushman Card is generated. Keep a printed or digital copy.
5. At the hospital, show the card. Treatment is cashless.

## Important points

- Treatment must be at an empanelled hospital for cashless benefit.
- For emergencies the scheme can be used first and paperwork completed later.
- Helpline 14555 and 1800-11-1565 handle complaints about denial of treatment.
