## What is MGNREGA

The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act gives every rural household a legal
right to at least 100 days of unskilled wage employment in a financial year. It is a demand-driven
scheme: work is provided when you ask for it, within 15 days.

## Key entitlements

- 100 days of guaranteed wage employment per rural household per financial year.
- Work within 5 km of your village. If work is given beyond 5 km, 10% extra wages are payable.
- Wages must be paid within 15 days of work. If delayed, the worker is entitled to compensation.
- If work is not provided within 15 days of demanding it, an unemployment allowance is payable.
- Equal wages for men and women. At least one third of workers must be women.
- Drinking water, shade and first aid must be provided at the work site.

## How to get a job card

1. Apply to the Gram Panchayat in writing or orally for a job card.
2. The Panchayat registers the household and issues a job card within 15 days.
3. The job card has photographs of adult members and is free of cost.
4. Keep the job card safely. It is the record of every day worked and wages due.

## How to demand work

1. Submit a written application to the Gram Panchayat asking for work, or apply online.
2. Keep a receipt of the application. This receipt proves the date you demanded work.
3. Work must be allotted within 15 days. The muster roll records attendance.
4. Check your wage payment and attendance on nrega.nic.in. Complaints about unpaid wages can be
   made to the programme officer or on the helpline.

## हिंदी में सारांश

- मनरेगा में हर ग्रामीण परिवार को साल में कम से कम 100 दिन के रोजगार का कानूनी अधिकार है।
- काम मांगने पर 15 दिन के अंदर मिलना चाहिए, वरना बेरोजगारी भत्ता मिलता है।
- मजदूरी 15 दिन के अंदर बैंक खाते में आनी चाहिए। देरी होने पर मुआवजा मिलता है।
- जॉब कार्ड ग्राम पंचायत से मुफ्त बनता है, 15 दिन में।
- काम गांव से 5 किलोमीटर के अंदर मिलना चाहिए, दूर होने पर 10 प्रतिशत अतिरिक्त मजदूरी।
- हजारी और भुगतान nrega.nic.in पर देख सकते हैं।
