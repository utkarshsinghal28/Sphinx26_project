## What is KCC

The Kisan Credit Card is a revolving credit facility for farmers. It gives short-term credit for
inputs like seed, fertiliser and pesticides, and for post-harvest and consumption needs. It works
like an overdraft limit: you draw money when you need it and repay after harvest.

## Loan limit and interest

- Short-term crop loans up to Rs 3 lakh are available at 7% interest per year.
- If you repay on time, the government gives a 3% prompt repayment incentive, which makes the
  effective interest rate 4% per year.
- The credit limit is fixed based on land holding, crop grown and scale of finance for the area.
- The limit is sanctioned for up to 5 years and can be reviewed. No fresh documentation every year.

## Security / collateral

- Loans up to Rs 1.60 lakh are given without any collateral security.
- For higher amounts the bank may ask for security, but the credit guarantee is provided by the
  government for eligible farmers.

## How to apply

1. Visit the nearest bank branch (any bank, including co-operative and regional rural banks).
2. Fill the KCC application form. Many banks allow online application.
3. Submit land documents and KYC.
4. The bank assesses the scale of finance and sanctions a limit.
5. The card / limit is issued. You can withdraw with a card, chequebook or through mobile banking.

## Documents needed

- Aadhaar card and PAN
- Land ownership documents
- Passport size photographs
- Bank passbook or account details
- Crop sowing details for the season

## हिंदी में सारांश

- किसान क्रेडिट कार्ड से 3 लाख रुपये तक कम ब्याज पर कर्ज मिलता है।
- समय पर भुगतान करने पर ब्याज 7 प्रतिशत से घटकर 4 प्रतिशत प्रभावी हो जाता है।
- 1.60 लाख रुपये तक के कर्ज के लिए कोई गारंटी (कोलैटरल) नहीं देनी पड़ती।
- आवेदन नजदीकी बैंक शाखा में करें। आधार, पैन, जमीन के कागजात और फोटो चाहिए।
- यह सीमा 5 साल तक वैध रहती है, हर साल नए कागजात नहीं देने पड़ते।
